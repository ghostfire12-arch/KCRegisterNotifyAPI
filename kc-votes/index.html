Polling Hours

All KC polling places are open 6 AM – 7 PM. In line by 7 PM? You have the right to vote.

Photo ID Required

Bring a MO driver's license, passport, or any government-issued photo ID. First-timers may use a utility bill or bank statement.

Vote Absentee

No reason needed. Request an absentee ballot at least 6 days before the election, or vote absentee in-person at 2841 McGee Trafficway.
